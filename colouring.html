Colouring Studio

The Colouring Studio adds art to language learning. A learner colours African-themed pictures, saves the finished work, and earns 15 XP for each saved artwork. There are 30 pictures in the studio.

Fill

Tap a closed area to fill it with colour, for a quick and tidy finish.

Brush

Paint freely with a brush, for shading and detail around the edges.

Save and earn

Undo a step if it goes wrong, then save the artwork to earn 15 XP.

How to use it

  1. Pick a picture from the studio.
  2. Choose Fill for flat areas, or Brush for free painting.
  3. Use Undo if a stroke or fill goes wrong.
  4. Save the finished artwork to add 15 XP to your progress.

In the demo, saved artworks stay on your own device. They are not shared with other learners from the demo alone.

Colouring is one way to stay in the language for a few extra minutes each day, and it counts toward the 30 XP daily goal. See progress for the full XP table.

Daily goal

Earn 30 XP a day to keep your streak alive. A finished story gives 25 XP, a quiz point gives 10 XP, and a saved artwork gives 15 XP.
