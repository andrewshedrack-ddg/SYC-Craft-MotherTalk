About MotherTalk

Many young people in African cities hear their mother tongue at home but rarely read it, speak it in full sentences, or pass it on. MotherTalk answers that gap with stories. A learner does not study a language from a distance. The learner walks with a character, greets people, buys food, crosses a lake, or visits a farm, and learns the words that belong to that day.

What MotherTalk does

  • Tells branching stories in Kikuyu, Kamba, Swahili, Dholuo, and Luganda.
  • Adds short quizzes after scenes, so a learner checks what was understood.
  • Offers Listen and Practise voice buttons, so a learner hears the language and practises it slowly.
  • Adds a Colouring Studio with 30 pictures, for learning through art as well as words.
  • Tracks XP, streaks, and levels, so small daily progress is easy to see.

An honest note

The story phrases in the demo are samples. They still await review by native speakers, so they should be read as learning samples, not as a finished language course. The aim of this site is to show the design clearly, with that limit stated plainly.

Daily goal

Earn 30 XP a day to keep your streak alive. A finished story gives 25 XP, a quiz point gives 10 XP, and a saved artwork gives 15 XP.
